BEFORE YOU START

A few practical things.

What if my computer doesn’t turn on?

You can select “Not powering on” during donation. The team still needs to inspect it before deciding whether it can be refurbished. Devices with swollen, leaking, damaged, or recalled batteries aren’t accepted.

What happens to my files?

Back up anything you want to keep, sign out, and remove activation locks. The team must record a data wipe before a device is offered to another student. This app tracks that process; it does not erase a physical device itself.

Who can reserve a computer?

Provo-area college students. Browse the catalog, confirm student eligibility, and reserve a device for local pickup. Any listed price is due at pickup. No online payment is collected.

Can I try the full process for class?

Yes. Sign in using a fictional email and open the test sign-in link. Create a donation, then use the classroom controls in its details to move it through refurbishment. A second account can reserve and collect it, unlocking the donor’s reward.

Is this an official BYU program?

No. ByteBack is an independent academic project, not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by Brigham Young University.
